We study \textbf{OD-conditioned trajectory generation with a known destination} (KnownDestination) under an explicit temporal semantics setting (dt-fixed $\Delta t = 30$s). We investigate the following questions under a strict, leakage-free evaluation protocol:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Coverage vs.\ realism:} how do deterministic regression and diffusion-based generators compare in micro-level errors (mean and best-of-$K$) and macro-level motion statistics (MSD/Rog)?
  \item \textbf{Main hypothesis:} can a \textbf{residual decomposition} (deterministic prior + stochastic residual diffusion) mitigate macroscopic shrinkage while retaining multi-modality?
  \item \textbf{Role of physics conditioning:} does injecting a \textbf{train-only navigation-field prior} improve best-of-$K$ coverage without introducing conservative bias in macroscopic motion?
\end{itemize}
