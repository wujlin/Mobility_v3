We study \textbf{OD-conditioned trajectory generation with a known destination} (KnownDestination) under an explicit temporal semantics setting (dt-fixed $\Delta t = 30$s). The central question is:
\begin{quote}
\emph{Can a generative model produce \textbf{multi-modal} future trajectories (good best-of-$K$ coverage) while remaining \textbf{macroscopically physically consistent} (realistic displacement and speed statistics) under a strict, leakage-free evaluation protocol?}
\end{quote}
We further ask whether injecting a \textbf{train-only navigation-field prior} improves coverage without sacrificing macroscopic realism, and whether a \textbf{residual decomposition} (deterministic prior + stochastic residual diffusion) can structurally resolve the shrinkage artifact observed in pure diffusion.
