We use the Shenzhen taxicab GPS dataset \citep{zhang2015urbancps} and preprocess it into a strict, reproducible dt-fixed benchmark.

\paragraph{Study region and grid representation.}
We discretize the city bounding box (latitude $[22.45, 22.85]$, longitude $[113.75, 114.65]$) into a $H_g \times W_g = 400 \times 800$ grid. Trajectory positions are stored as grid coordinates $\mathbf{p}_t=[y_t,x_t]$.

\paragraph{dt-fixed resampling and splits.}
To assign explicit time semantics to displacements, we resample trajectories to a fixed interval $\Delta t = 30$s and drop segments with large gaps or insufficient length. The resulting split sizes are train=97,222, val=21,103, and test=21,309 trajectories.

\paragraph{Leakage-free data products.}
All normalization statistics and the navigation field (used for conditioning) are computed using the \textbf{train split only}. This strict contract prevents information leakage from validation/test into conditioning signals or scaling constants.

\paragraph{Windows and conditioning.}
We use an observation window length $H=8$ and prediction horizon $F=12$ (4 minutes of history and 6 minutes of future). The global conditioning vector includes time features (hour-of-day, day-of-week) and normalized origin/destination (OD) endpoints.
