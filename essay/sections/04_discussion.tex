\paragraph{Micro--macro tension in generative mobility modeling.}
Our results highlight a consistent trade-off: deterministic regression minimizes mean error but collapses multi-modality, while diffusion improves best-of-$K$ coverage yet can violate macroscopic realism. In our dt-fixed setting, this appears as \emph{macroscopic shrinkage}: diffusion samples systematically under-estimate net displacement and speed, which we quantify as lower MSD/Rog.

\paragraph{Why shrinkage happens and what navigation field conditioning changes.}
Diffusion sampling refines a noisy velocity sequence through multiple denoising steps. When the local direction is uncertain, the learned conditional distribution can favor conservative updates (smaller displacements or averaged directions). These step-level biases accumulate over the horizon and reduce net displacement, which appears as lower MSD/Rog. Navigation field conditioning provides a data-derived local mean direction and speed cue, which can improve best-of-$K$ coverage; at the same time, because it represents an average flow, conditioning can bias samples toward conservative macroscopic motion relative to the ground truth.

\paragraph{Residual diffusion as a structural fix.}
Residual decomposition separates responsibilities: the deterministic prior carries OD-driven macroscopic motion scale, while diffusion focuses on stochastic deviations around that prior. This targets shrinkage directly, because net displacement scale is provided by the prior rather than being learned implicitly through the denoising process.

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.}
Our current pipeline is grid-based and uses a simple bbox projection for geographic visualization, without road-level map matching or explicit network constraints. Future work should incorporate road-graph structure, and explore adaptive conditioning mechanisms (e.g., learnable gating) to use the navigation field when helpful without over-constraining macroscopic motion. Finally, stronger conclusions require larger-scale evaluation and multi-seed replication under the same strict data contract.
