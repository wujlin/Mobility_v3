We study long-horizon route generation and identify corridor-level multi-modality as a root cause of destructive averaging and mode interference in end-to-end coordinate generators.
We propose CascadeTraj, a decision--execution generator that makes corridor commitments explicit via waypoint skeleton sampling, and show that commitment-preserving execution is critical for successful composition.
Future work will expand benchmarking across cities, strengthen realism/feasibility evaluations, and explore richer semantic controls while keeping route generation as the central objective.
